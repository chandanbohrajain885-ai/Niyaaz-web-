Our Story

One handi. One recipe.
Since 1989.

Niyaaz began as a small eatery feeding travellers in Belagavi. The biryani did the marketing — meat marinated and layered with long-grain rice, sealed and slow-cooked on dum until every grain carries the masala.

Today the Original Belgaum Biryani is served across Belagavi, Goa and Kolhapur — and it is still made the way it was on day one.
