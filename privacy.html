Privacy Policy

What we collect. When you order, reserve, request catering, join the Royal Club or send feedback, we collect your name, mobile number, the branch, your order details and (for delivery) your address.


Why. To prepare and deliver your order, confirm bookings, calculate Royal Points, respond to feedback and, if you are a past customer, occasionally send you offers on WhatsApp. You can ask us to stop at any time.


Sharing. We never sell your data. It is stored with our hosting and database providers only to run this service.


Your rights. You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Contact your nearest branch or message us on WhatsApp.
